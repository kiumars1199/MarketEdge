# Kismet

> Kismet shows prediction-market traders which contracts are priced below their estimated win chance, after the
> fee and after the cost of crossing the spread. Every number comes with its basis: sample size, model level and the
> walk-forward backtest record. Flags are the same for everyone; Kismet gives no personal advice and places no bets.

Market analytics, not financial advice. Based on past results. 18+.

## Public pages

- [How it works](http://localhost:3161/about): the model, the price check and what the numbers mean
- [Track record](http://localhost:3161/track-record): walk-forward backtest, calibration and the live settled-bet record
- [Pricing](http://localhost:3161/pricing): Free, Pro and Premium
- [Terms](http://localhost:3161/terms), [Privacy](http://localhost:3161/privacy), [Refunds](http://localhost:3161/refunds), [Disclaimer](http://localhost:3161/disclaimer), [Contact](http://localhost:3161/contact)

## Use Kismet in an AI assistant (MCP)

- Server URL: http://localhost:3161/api/mcp (streamable HTTP, OAuth 2.1 sign-in with your Kismet account)
- Read-only tools: best_bets, search_markets, get_market, track_record, my_list
- Plans: Premium (up to 60 calls a minute); Pro includes 3 calls a day; not on Free
- Setup steps for Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor: http://localhost:3161/account (signed in)

## For crawlers

Market data, Best bets and account pages need a signed-in account and aren't for automated reading. Training
crawlers are not allowed anywhere (see http://localhost:3161/robots.txt).
